# Cover6 First Watch — 7-Day Cyber Kill Chain Investigation

First Watch is a seven-day cybersecurity investigation series developed by [Cover6 Solutions](https://www.cover6solutions.com/). The exercise follows the Lockheed Martin Cyber Kill Chain, providing an opportunity to investigate activity across the different phases of a simulated intrusion.

Throughout the series, I analyze network traffic and security logs using tools such as Wireshark and Splunk to identify suspicious activity, correlate evidence, and develop an understanding of how an intrusion progresses.

The objective is to strengthen my investigative skills, practice evidence-based analysis, and understand how observations from individual stages can contribute to a broader incident investigation.

## Objectives

- Analyze network traffic and security logs to identify suspicious activity.

- Develop investigative skills across the seven phases of the Cyber Kill Chain.

- Correlate evidence from different sources to build an incident timeline.

- Distinguish observed activity from assumptions and unsupported conclusions.

- Document findings and investigative reasoning.

## Investigation Series

The project is organized into seven daily activities, each focused on a phase of the Cyber Kill Chain.

Each day includes investigation notes, key findings, and lessons learned. A companion blog series, titled [**Cover6 First Watch: Field Notes**](#), documents my experience and investigative thought process as I progress through the exercise.
