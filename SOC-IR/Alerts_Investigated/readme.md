# Investigated Alerts

This section contains hands-on alert investigations completed through cybersecurity training platforms and lab exercises, including LetsDefend and MYDFIR.

Each investigation documents the alert details, relevant findings, investigative summary, analyst assessment, and recommended next steps. The focus is on analyzing available evidence, correlating activity across relevant telemetry, distinguishing malicious behavior from benign activity, and reaching defensible conclusions.

These exercises demonstrate practical SOC investigation skills and the application of analytical techniques in simulated environments. They represent independent learning and hands-on practice, not professional incident-response engagements.

When appropriate, I will also add a companion blog post for an alert where I expand on my investigation thought process, challenges, and lessons learned.
